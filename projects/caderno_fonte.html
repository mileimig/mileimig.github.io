Fonte da Liberdade is a training and self-discovery program aimed at women and leaderships facing social vulnerability. In Brazil, the program is the result of a partnership between the Freedom Fund, an international organization dedicated to ending modern slavery, and the Instituto Fonte, a Brazilian organization specializing in social and human development.

Created by a team of three designers, the editorial project Fonte da Liberdade: Inspirações, Práticas e Aprendizagens translated the program’s content into a two-volume collection of over 400 pages, combining texts and exercises in a visually engaging and accessible format. The work invites reflection on how design can serve as a powerful tool in social initiatives, supporting knowledge sharing and community empowerment, strengthening the program’s identity and broadening its reach across various educational contexts.
