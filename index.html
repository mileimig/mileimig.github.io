Milena Leimig is a designer and multidisciplinary artist creating across image, type and interaction ✽

( 1 ) Practice

My work is centered around experimenting in visual identities and narratives, interaction design (web, mobile and product interfaces), illustrations, typography, frame-by-frame animation, and 3D.

( 2 ) About

Drawing since a young age, I graduated in Design and have been researching the intersections of semiotics, visual heritage, and language. My practice includes digital and traditional mediums, creating systems and making small games.
